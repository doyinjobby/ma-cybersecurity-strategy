# Post-Acquisition Cybersecurity Integration

## Purpose

This section demonstrates a structured, risk-based approach to integrating cybersecurity capabilities following a merger or acquisition.

The objective is to transition identified due-diligence risks into prioritised security actions while maintaining business continuity, protecting critical information assets and progressively aligning the acquired organisation with the acquiring organisation's security governance framework.

---

## Integration Approach

The post-acquisition cybersecurity integration programme is structured across three phases:

### Days 0–30 — Assess, Stabilise and Prioritise

Focus on establishing visibility, validating critical assets and access, assessing immediate security exposure and prioritising risks requiring early intervention.

### Days 31–60 — Align, Remediate and Integrate

Focus on harmonising security policies, integrating identity and access management, remediating priority vulnerabilities, strengthening resilience and aligning security practices across the combined organisation.

### Days 61–90 — Assure, Govern and Optimise

Focus on ISMS integration, security assurance, penetration testing, executive reporting, management review and establishing the transition into continuous security improvement.
