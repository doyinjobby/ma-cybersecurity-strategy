## Overview

This repository demonstrates a practical cybersecurity strategy for supporting mergers and acquisitions (M&A) from pre-acquisition due diligence through post-acquisition security integration.

It showcases governance, risk management, third-party assurance, contractual security, executive reporting, and business resilience deliverables commonly used during M&A engagements.

---

## Objectives

- Identify cybersecurity risks before acquisition
- Evaluate the maturity of security controls
- Support informed investment decisions
- Develop a post-acquisition integration roadmap
- Reduce operational, regulatory, and cyber risk
- Improve organisational resilience
