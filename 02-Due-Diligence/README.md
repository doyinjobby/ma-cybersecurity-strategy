# Cybersecurity Due Diligence

## Purpose

Cybersecurity due diligence identifies security, privacy, operational, regulatory and technology risks before an acquisition is completed.

The objective is to determine whether cybersecurity risks could affect the value of the transaction or require remediation before or after acquisition.

---

## Assessment Areas

- Governance
- Security Policies
- ISO 27001 Maturity
- SOC 2 Reports
- Cloud Security
- Third-Party Risk
- Identity & Access Management
- Vulnerability Management
- Incident Response
- Business Continuity
- Data Protection
- Regulatory Compliance

---

## Deliverables

- Due Diligence Report
- Vendor Assessment
- Risk Register
- Executive Summary
- Integration Recommendations
