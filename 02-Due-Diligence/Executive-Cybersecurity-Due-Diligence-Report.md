# Executive Summary

This report presents the findings of a cybersecurity due diligence assessment conducted on a target organisation as part of a proposed merger or acquisition (M&A). The objective is to evaluate the organisation's cybersecurity maturity, identify material cyber risks, assess regulatory compliance, and provide recommendations to support informed investment decisions and secure post-acquisition integration.

The assessment focuses on governance, security controls, cloud security, third-party risk, business continuity, regulatory compliance, and operational resilience.

Overall Security Rating:

**Moderate Risk**

---

# Objectives

The objectives of this assessment are to:

- Evaluate cybersecurity governance maturity
- Identify risks that may impact the transaction
- Review security controls against recognised frameworks
- Assess regulatory compliance obligations
- Estimate remediation priorities
- Support executive decision-making
- Develop an initial post-acquisition integration roadmap

# Key Strengths

The organisation demonstrates several areas of good security practice:

- Information Security policies are documented.
- Executive leadership supports cybersecurity initiatives.
- Security awareness training is delivered regularly.
- Incident response procedures are established.
- Risk assessments are performed periodically.
- Cloud security controls have been implemented.
- Regulatory obligations have been identified.

---

# Key Risks

The assessment identified several areas requiring improvement:

| Risk | Impact | Priority |
|------|---------|----------|
| Inconsistent supplier assurance | High | High |
| Limited continuous control monitoring | Medium | Medium |
| Incomplete asset inventory | Medium | Medium |
| Privileged access reviews require improvement | Medium | Medium |
| Security metrics require standardisation | Low | Medium |

---

# Third-Party Risk Assessment

The organisation relies on several external suppliers to provide critical business services.

Key observations include:

- Vendor security assessments are performed.
- Contractual security clauses are partially implemented.
- Supplier assurance evidence is collected.
- Continuous monitoring should be strengthened.
- Critical suppliers should undergo periodic reassessment.

# Recommendations

## Immediate (0–30 Days)

- Validate privileged accounts
- Review administrator access
- Confirm backup integrity
- Review critical suppliers
- Validate endpoint protection coverage

## Short-Term (30–60 Days)

- Harmonise security policies
- Review cloud configurations
- Update risk register
- Perform supplier reassessments
- Standardise security metrics

## Medium-Term (60–90 Days)

- Integrate into enterprise ISMS
- Align controls with ISO 27001
- Complete remediation activities
- Perform executive management review
- Update Business Continuity Plans

---

# Conclusion

The target organisation demonstrates a generally mature cybersecurity capability with no critical findings that would prevent the proposed acquisition.

Several medium-priority improvements have been identified relating to supplier assurance, governance maturity, and continuous security monitoring. These risks can be effectively managed through a structured post-acquisition integration programme.

Overall, the organisation is considered suitable for acquisition subject to completion of the recommended remediation activities and ongoing security governance.
