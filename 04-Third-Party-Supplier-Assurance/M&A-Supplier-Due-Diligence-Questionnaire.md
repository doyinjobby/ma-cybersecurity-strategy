# M&A Supplier Cybersecurity Due Diligence Questionnaire

**Author:** Dr. Olajumoke Bello, PhD  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Classification:** Public Portfolio Sample  
**Status:** Illustrative M&A Third-Party Assurance Deliverable

---

## 1. Purpose

This questionnaire provides a structured approach for assessing cybersecurity, information security, privacy and operational resilience risks associated with critical third-party suppliers during merger and acquisition (M&A) due diligence.

The questionnaire is designed to support risk-based supplier assurance by identifying control weaknesses, dependencies and contractual risks that may affect the transaction or require remediation during post-acquisition integration.

The depth of assessment should be proportionate to supplier criticality and risk tier.

---

## 2. Supplier Profile and Criticality

1. What services does the supplier provide?
2. Which business processes depend on the supplier?
3. Which systems, applications or infrastructure does the supplier support?
4. Does the supplier access, process, store or transmit organisational information?
5. What classifications of information are involved?
6. Does the supplier process personal or regulated data?
7. Does the supplier have network, remote, privileged or administrative access?
8. Which geographic locations are used to deliver the service?
9. Where is organisational data stored or processed?
10. Could failure of the supplier materially disrupt a critical business service?
11. Are viable alternative suppliers available?
12. Does the supplier rely on material subcontractors or fourth parties?

---

## 3. Information Security Governance

Assessment questions include:

- Does the supplier maintain a documented information security programme?
- Is there an accountable senior owner for information security?
- Are security policies formally approved and periodically reviewed?
- Is cybersecurity risk assessed and managed through a documented process?
- Are security roles and responsibilities clearly defined?
- Does the supplier conduct periodic internal security assessments or audits?
- Are material security risks reported to senior management?

### Evidence Examples

- Information Security Policy
- Security governance structure
- Risk-management methodology
- Security risk register
- Internal audit reports
- Management review evidence

---

## 4. Security Certifications and Independent Assurance

Assessment questions include:

- Is the supplier certified to ISO/IEC 27001?
- What is the scope of the certification?
- Is the certification current?
- Has the supplier obtained SOC 2 or equivalent independent assurance?
- Where applicable, is a SOC 2 Type II report available?
- Are material findings or exceptions identified in assurance reports?
- Are remediation actions being tracked?

### Evidence Examples

- ISO 27001 certificate
- Certification scope
- Statement of Applicability where appropriate
- SOC 2 report
- Independent audit reports
- Remediation evidence

---

## 5. Identity and Access Management

Assessment questions include:

- How are user identities created, modified and removed?
- Is Multi-Factor Authentication used for privileged and remote access?
- How are privileged accounts controlled?
- Are access rights periodically reviewed?
- Are dormant or unnecessary accounts removed?
- Is segregation of duties implemented where appropriate?
- How is third-party access monitored?
- Are privileged activities logged?

### Evidence Examples

- IAM policy
- Access-control procedure
- Access review evidence
- Privileged Access Management controls
- MFA configuration evidence
- Joiner-Mover-Leaver process

---

## 6. Data Protection and Privacy

Assessment questions include:

- What personal or sensitive information does the supplier process?
- What is the lawful basis or contractual basis for processing?
- Where is information stored and processed?
- Are international data transfers involved?
- Are retention and deletion requirements defined?
- Are appropriate technical and organisational measures implemented?
- Are data-processing agreements in place where required?
- Are subprocessors identified and governed?
- How are data-subject rights supported?
- Has the supplier experienced material privacy incidents?

### Evidence Examples

- Data Processing Agreement
- Data-flow documentation
- DPIA where applicable
- Retention schedule
- Subprocessor register
- Privacy policies

---

## 7. Vulnerability and Security Testing

Assessment questions include:

- Does the supplier maintain a vulnerability-management programme?
- How frequently are vulnerability scans performed?
- How are vulnerabilities prioritised?
- Are remediation timescales defined according to severity?
- Are critical vulnerabilities tracked to closure?
- Are penetration tests conducted periodically?
- Are material penetration-test findings remediated?
- How are security patches managed?

### Evidence Examples

- Vulnerability-management policy
- Vulnerability scan summary
- Remediation SLA
- Penetration-test executive summary
- Patch-management procedure
- Remediation tracker

---

## 8. Cloud and Infrastructure Security

Assessment questions include:

- Which cloud or hosting providers support the service?
- How is cloud access controlled?
- Are cloud configurations periodically assessed?
- Is encryption implemented for sensitive data at rest and in transit?
- Are security logs collected and monitored?
- Are production environments appropriately segregated?
- How are security baselines established?
- Are cloud responsibilities between the supplier and cloud provider clearly defined?

### Evidence Examples

- Cloud architecture documentation
- Security configuration standards
- Encryption controls
- Logging and monitoring evidence
- Cloud security assessment
- Shared-responsibility documentation

---

## 9. Security Monitoring and Incident Response

Assessment questions include:

- Does the supplier maintain a documented incident-response plan?
- Are security events centrally monitored?
- Are incident severity and escalation criteria defined?
- Are security incidents formally recorded and investigated?
- Has the supplier experienced material cybersecurity incidents?
- How quickly must the supplier notify customers of relevant incidents?
- Are incident-response exercises conducted?
- Are lessons learned incorporated into security improvements?

### Evidence Examples

- Incident Response Plan
- Incident register
- Escalation procedure
- Exercise or tabletop-test results
- Security monitoring documentation
- Incident-notification procedure

---

## 10. Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery

Assessment questions include:

- Does the supplier maintain documented business-continuity arrangements?
- Has a Business Impact Analysis been completed?
- Are Recovery Time Objectives defined?
- Are Recovery Point Objectives defined?
- Are backups appropriately protected?
- Are recovery procedures periodically tested?
- When was the most recent disaster-recovery test?
- Were material issues identified during testing?
- Are critical fourth-party dependencies included in continuity planning?

### Evidence Examples

- Business Continuity Plan
- Business Impact Analysis
- Disaster Recovery Plan
- RTO/RPO documentation
- Backup and recovery evidence
- Continuity exercise results

---

## 11. Fourth-Party and Supply Chain Risk

Assessment questions include:

- Does the supplier maintain an inventory of material subcontractors?
- Which fourth parties support delivery of the service?
- Do fourth parties access organisational information?
- How does the supplier assess subcontractor security?
- Are security requirements flowed down contractually?
- Are customers notified before material subprocessors are changed?
- Are concentration risks associated with common service providers understood?
- How are fourth-party incidents managed?

### Evidence Examples

- Subcontractor register
- Subprocessor list
- Fourth-party assessment process
- Contractual flow-down requirements
- Supplier dependency documentation

---

## 12. Contractual Security Requirements

Assessment questions include:

- Does the contract contain appropriate information-security obligations?
- Are data-protection responsibilities defined?
- Are security-incident notification timescales specified?
- Does the organisation have appropriate audit or assurance rights?
- Are subcontractor requirements defined?
- Are business-continuity obligations included where required?
- Are data return and secure-deletion requirements defined?
- Are termination and exit-security requirements addressed?
- Are material security changes subject to notification?

### Evidence Examples

- Master Services Agreement
- Data Processing Agreement
- Security schedule
- Service Level Agreement
- Business-continuity clauses
- Termination and exit provisions

---

## 13. M&A Risk Evaluation

Material supplier findings should be assessed according to:

- Supplier criticality
- Likelihood of risk occurrence
- Business and operational impact
- Cybersecurity impact
- Regulatory and privacy exposure
- Contractual exposure
- Availability of alternative suppliers
- Concentration risk
- Fourth-party dependency
- Remediation complexity

Findings should be recorded in the M&A Cybersecurity Risk Register where they create material transaction or integration risk.

---

## 14. Deal-Impact Classification

Material findings may be classified as:

- **Deal Breaker** — supplier risk creates unacceptable transaction exposure
- **Pre-Close Condition** — remediation or contractual protection required before completion
- **Day-1 Priority** — immediate post-acquisition action required
- **30–90 Day Remediation** — remediation can be managed during integration
- **Longer-Term Improvement** — finding can transition into ongoing supplier governance

---

## 15. Assessment Outcome

Following assessment, the supplier should have:

- An assigned risk tier
- Documented assurance evidence
- Identified control gaps
- Recorded cybersecurity and resilience risks
- Agreed remediation actions
- Named risk and business owners
- Target remediation dates
- Residual risk assessment
- Appropriate escalation where required
- Defined reassessment or ongoing-monitoring requirements

The assessment lifecycle is:

**Supplier Identification → Risk Tiering → Due Diligence → Evidence Review → Risk Assessment → Deal Impact → Remediation → Ongoing Assurance**
