## Purpose

This section demonstrates a structured, risk-based approach to assessing and managing third-party and supplier cybersecurity risk during mergers and acquisitions.

Third-party relationships can introduce significant cybersecurity, privacy, operational and resilience dependencies during an acquisition. These risks may arise from suppliers with access to sensitive information, critical systems, cloud environments, outsourced services or essential business processes.

The objective is to identify material supplier risks during due diligence, determine their potential impact on the transaction and establish appropriate assurance, remediation, contractual and post-acquisition integration actions.

---

## Key Assessment Areas

- Supplier inventory and dependency identification
- Supplier criticality and risk tiering
- Cybersecurity due diligence
- ISO 27001 and SOC 2 assurance evidence
- Data protection and privacy
- Access to systems and sensitive information
- Cloud and outsourced service dependencies
- Contractual security requirements
- Incident and breach notification obligations
- Business continuity and operational resilience
- Concentration risk
- Fourth-party dependencies
- Remediation and risk acceptance

## Relationship to the M&A Lifecycle

Third-party assurance should operate throughout the acquisition lifecycle:

**Supplier Discovery → Criticality Assessment → Due Diligence → Risk Assessment → Deal Impact → Contract Review → Remediation → Post-Acquisition Assurance**

This provides a traceable approach for identifying supplier dependencies before acquisition and managing material third-party risks throughout integration.
